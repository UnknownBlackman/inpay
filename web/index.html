Questions merchants ask us

short answers, promise

Do I ever hold crypto?

No. The coin sits in the escrow contract and goes to the payout provider. You only ever see money in your own currency.

What does it cost my customer?

The provider's conversion fee, between 0.8% and 2.5%, shown on the checkout before they pay.

What if they send the wrong amount?

Extra comes straight back. Too little, and they see exactly what's missing; if they don't top up in 10 minutes, everything is refunded.

Can I use it with real money today?

Not yet. It runs on NEAR testnet, with simulated payout providers and banks.
